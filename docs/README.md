## Historical records

The [end-to-end audit](E2E_AUDIT.md) and [repository readiness review](REPOSITORY_READINESS.md) retain dates, measurements, resolved findings, and limitations. Earlier phase reviews are historical records. Statements such as “not yet published” or “license not selected” describe those review dates. Current scope is defined by the root README, LICENSE, and changelog. Original source and evidence hashes remain unchanged.

The former Atlas name appears in historical records and internal prompts. Threadline is the public platform name. A rename does not convert an earlier test into a new evaluation run.
