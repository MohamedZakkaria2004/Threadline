# HR onboarding checklist

HR creates a new starter record at least 5 working days before the joining date. The onboarding coordinator verifies the signed offer and emergency contact details, then requests an IT account and equipment. The new starter attends orientation within the first 2 working days. The line manager completes the role induction checklist within 10 working days. HR keeps the signed checklist in the restricted personnel record. This procedure contains synthetic demonstration requirements.
