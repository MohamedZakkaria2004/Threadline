# Network incident response

A priority-one network incident must be acknowledged within 15 minutes. The incident commander publishes a status update every 30 minutes until service is restored. The on-call engineer records the affected service, start time, symptoms, and immediate containment steps. The operations manager approves external customer updates. A post-incident review is completed within 3 business days after restoration. Incident priority is based on service impact, not on the seniority of the requester.
