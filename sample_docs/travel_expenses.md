# Domestic travel and expenses

For approved domestic business travel, the meal reimbursement limit is AED 150 per person per day. The domestic hotel limit is AED 750 per night. Employees must attach itemized receipts and submit their expense claim within 7 calendar days after returning. The line manager approves the business purpose before travel. Finance reviews the receipts before reimbursement. Personal entertainment and expenses without receipts are not reimbursable under this policy.
