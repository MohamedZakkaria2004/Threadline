# Purchase request procedure

Employees submit purchase requests with a business justification and a supplier quotation. Requests up to AED 500 require line-manager approval. Requests above AED 500 and up to AED 5000 require Procurement approval. Requests above AED 5000 require both Procurement and Finance approval. Split purchases intended to avoid an approval threshold are prohibited. The requester must wait for approval before placing an order. This application only explains the policy; it cannot place orders.
