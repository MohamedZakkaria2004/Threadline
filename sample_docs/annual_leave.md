# Annual leave policy

Permanent employees receive 25 working days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave must be requested in the HR portal at least 10 working days before the start date. The employee's line manager approves annual leave. Employees may carry forward up to 5 unused working days, which must be used by 31 March of the following year. Unused carried days expire after that date. This policy applies to permanent employees of the fictional Meridian Telecom company.
